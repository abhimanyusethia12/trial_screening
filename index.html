The model goes to the data

Good pre-screening needs the full health record. Instead of sending the record to a recruiter, Prescreen brings the check to the record.

1

Add a health record

The patient opens their own record in the standard FHIR format. It is read on the device and never uploaded.

2

Checked on the device

Rules check diagnoses, dated lab results and medicines. An on-device model reads clinic notes and cites the sentence behind every answer. Only gaps become plain-language questions.

3

Shared only by choice

The patient sees which trials they may qualify for and why. If they consent, the site receives a per-criterion summary, never the record.

For sites and sponsors

Referrals arrive pre-screened, with the evidence for each criterion, so coordinators call the patients most likely to enrol.

See Console Demo
  • A referral inbox with evidenceEvery criterion shows how it was met: from the record, from clinic notes, from the patient’s answer, or left for the screening visit.
  • Trials you controlPublish, pause or resume a trial, and import a new one from ClinicalTrials.gov by its NCT ID. Devices check against the current list, with no app update.
  • An anonymous funnelSee how many patients were checked, which criteria rule them out and where they drop off. No names or record values.

Safe by design

The AI can confirm a criterion but never rules a patient out on its own. If clinic notes suggest an exclusion, the patient sees the exact sentence and confirms it.
