Prescreen

On-device clinical trial pre-screening

Patients check their own health record against recruiting trials on their device. Sites receive verified referrals only when the patient chooses to send them.

Patient appMobile. Add a record, answer a few questions, see results and next steps. Site consoleReferrals to contact, trials and criteria, activity log.

Prototype. Patient records are synthetic. Trials are real ClinicalTrials.gov listings; criteria compiled by hand. Both apps share this browser’s storage in place of a server. Detailed evaluation view
